## MacOS Apps

### Currently Using

- [Brave Browser](https://brave.com/) #browser
  - A great balance between performance and features;
  - Built-in ad blocking and excellent vertical tabs;
  - Comes with many Web3-related buttons, but they can be disabled;
- [ChatWise](https://chatwise.app/) #chatbox
  - In my opinion, the best choice for a native macOS UI experience;
- [Dory](https://segev.sh/dory/) #switcher
  - Similar to [rcmd](https://lowtechguys.com/rcmd/), but with a cleaner feel;

- [Ghostty](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty) #terminal emulator
  - Comparable to WezTerm; minimalist and easy to configure;
- [Updatest](https://updatest.app/) #updater
  - An app updater that helped me migrate many of my apps to Homebrew management;

- [NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com/) #rss
  - A native, cross-platform RSS reader for macOS/iOS; clean, smooth, and feature-sufficient;

- [Velja](https://sindresorhus.com/velja) #browser picker
  - Automatically routes URLs to the appropriate browser based on custom rules;


### Watching

- [Helium Browser](https://github.com/imputnet/helium) #browser
  - A privacy-first alternative to the Chrome behemoth, though it lacks vertical tabs;
- [wezterm](https://github.com/wezterm/wezterm) #terminal emulator
  - The stable version hasn't been updated in a long time; the novelty has worn off😁;

- [Latest](https://max.codes/latest/) #updater
  - Free and open-source, but lacks the advanced features of the paid Updatest;

- [Orion](https://orionbrowser.com/) #browser
  - A WebKit-based browser; unfortunately, it's currently too buggy and updates are slow;

- [Zed](https://zed.dev/) #editor
  - A high-performance editor built in Rust; its extensibility and customization still lag behind VS Code;


### Abandoned

- [Cotypist](https://cotypist.app/) #ai
  - System-wide AI auto-completion;
  - Performance was mediocre and felt a bit intrusive;

- [Folo](https://folo.is/) #rss
  - Laggy UI and gimmicky features; it felt like the developers lost their focus;
  - Dropped it entirely after they went "all-in on AI" and redesigned the UI;
